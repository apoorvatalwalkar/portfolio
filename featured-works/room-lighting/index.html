Background

Context

For this project, I was given a basic Maya scene of a room with no lights or shaders. I was tasked with adding shaders and lights to create a focal point and a mood for the scene.

Getting Started

Finding Reference Photos

Before editing the file itself, I wanted to find references for the mood I was going for. I looked on Pinterest for images close to what I had in mind, which also helped when it came to creating realistic light effects. I wanted an inviting mood with warm lighting, and I wanted to include some musical elements since that's the vibe I'd go for myself.

Step One

Shading

The first thing I did was shade all the objects. I wanted everything to have a warm tone, so the walls are an off-white, the wood is a warmer tone, and the red is very warm as well. To keep the scene cohesive, I kept the posters off-white and red to match.

Media area, shaded

Posters, shaded to match

Step Two

Lighting

Lastly, I added lights to the scene. I placed a lot of small lights around the room to match the vibe of my reference images. I also used an area light mapped to a Photoshop file I made of colored rings, to mimic a sunset lamp, and added a soft blue light through the windows to give the impression of late evening.

Final

Results

The result is a very moody evening scene. I really enjoyed playing with lights in this project and tweaking them to see what kind of effects I could create.
