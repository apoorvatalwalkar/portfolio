Hi, I'm Apoorva

I'm a student who loves problem-solving and combining my passions for the creative arts and computer science.

Academically:

Currently, I'm in my senior year at Brown University, double majoring in Computer Science and Visual Arts. On top of Systems, Software Engineering, and Algorithms, I've focused my coursework largely in Computer Graphics and AI/ML. I'm also doing research on cameras and geometry in the Brown Visual Computing Lab. Additionally, I'm the Head Teaching Assistant for the Introduction to Computer Graphics course!

Outside of the Classroom:

Outside of academics, I'm a captain of Brown's Division I Fencing team, earning both NFC All-Star and All-Academic honors for the 2024-25 and 2025-26 season. I've been the Social Media Intern for the team for the past two years. I'm also the marketing chair for the Women in Computer Science club where I work to build a community for female Software Engineers. In my free time, you can probably find me in my studio working on a drawing or painting.
